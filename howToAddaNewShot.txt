Grant's foolproof 3 step process of looking at a new timeslice

1) Create the folder {machine}_shots/{machine}_{shotNum}.{time}
    e.g.: DIIID_shots/DIIID_147634.04565

2) Add the eqdsk mapping '{shotNum}.{time}':'{eqdskName}' to shotToEqdsk.py
    e.g.: Add '147634.04565':'g147634.04565' to DIIID_eqdskDict

3a) If you're trying to reproduce an experimental spectrum, use exp_scripts/runExperimentalCase.py to take in the phase information and estimate the spectrum

3b) If you're assuming a spectrum, either directly use setupInputFiles.py or use scan_scripts/createScanForShot.py
